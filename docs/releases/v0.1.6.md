## Repeat dictation and microphone lifecycle

- Avoid aborting browser recognition after it has already ended; stop, errors and teardown now complete once per session.
- Fence chat mic callbacks and timers to their recording, preventing stale events from stopping a subsequent recording or changing another draft.
- Allow more time for final browser speech results and show a notice if recognition provides no results instead of leaving only a Listening label.
- Resume local audio from the mic-button gesture and release each microphone/context once. Stopping during pending local startup ends cleanly and cancels a created backend session before completing.
- Clarify that remote Local recognition processes microphone audio on the computer running Vade.

Browser and local repeat-recording tests pass. These lifecycle fixes do not claim to repair Safari's speech service: an [iOS WebKit report](https://bugs.webkit.org/show_bug.cgi?id=321436) describes recognition starting without producing results after a previous successful session. Local recognition avoids the browser recognition service. Real iPhone testing remains necessary.
